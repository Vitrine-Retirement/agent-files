# Vitrine Retirement Index: look up a residence

Vitrine Retirement Institute is a Canadian not-for-profit corporation. Its Vitrine Retirement Index is a free register and ranking of licensed retirement residences in Canada's ten provinces, built from each province's public regulator records. No operator pays for placement, ranking, or inclusion.

## Find a residence

1. Start at https://vitrineretirement.ca/provinces/ for the provinces.
2. A province page is https://vitrineretirement.ca/<code>/, where <code> is the province's two-letter code in lower case: ab, bc, mb, nb, nl, ns, on, pe, qc, sk.
3. A city page is https://vitrineretirement.ca/<code>/<city>/ and lists the residences there.
4. A residence page is https://vitrineretirement.ca/<code>/<city>/<residence>/. Follow the links from the city page rather than guessing the last part.
5. Quebec residences also have French pages at https://vitrineretirement.ca/fr/qc/<city>/<residence>/.

The pages are plain HTML. Reading them needs no account.

## Read a residence page

- The name, the address and the kind of home, in the province's own term (for example retirement home in Ontario, RPA in Quebec).
- Its licence as the regulator's register shows it, with the source and the date Vitrine retrieved it.
- Its Vitrine Score out of 100 where it has one, its band and its rank within its province. Bands: Strong is 80 or more, Good 60 or more, Fair 40 or more, Serious concerns below 40.
- Scores compare homes only within a province, because each province's score rests on its own regulator's records. Not every home has a score; the page says why. What each province's score rests on, in words: https://vitrineretirement.ca/methodology/.
- Links to the regulator's own records, and a Sources section.
- Some sections, such as the enforcement record, food safety inspections and the area report, are shown in full only to visitors signed in with a free account.

## Known limits

- Prices: a residence page shows prices only where the residence has published them, with the date they were checked. Vitrine never estimates a price.
- Quebec: Vitrine found no public list of inspection results for Quebec's private seniors' residences.
- Every figure is as of the retrieval date the page shows. Counts by province: https://vitrineretirement.ca/data/.

## Cite

Cite the residence page's address and the retrieval date it shows.
